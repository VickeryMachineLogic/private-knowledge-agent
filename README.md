# Private Knowledge Agent

**Ask questions of *your* manuals, SOPs, and research — get answers with sources, not a public chatbot.**

The Private Knowledge Agent (Web Researcher) keeps a local corpus of the documents your business depends on and answers from that material only, citing where each claim came from. It runs on hardware you own, so sensitive files never leave the building — and the finished system belongs to you, not a cloud vendor.

## What it does for you

- **Local corpus** — manuals, handbooks, SOPs, and research packs indexed on your own machines
- **Plain-language questions** — ask the way you'd ask a colleague
- **Answers with sources** — every claim traces back to the page it came from
- **Claim audit** — check answers against the source before you act on them
- **Local / graph RAG** — retrieval that stays private by default
- **Owned end to end** — no black-box lock-in, little or no per-token cost

## Who it's for

Teams buried in thick SOP folders, private research, or policy documents that can't be pasted into a public chatbot.
